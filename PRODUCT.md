# Product Overview: The Great Ledger

## Core Concept
A cozy, gamified RPG habit tracker and adventure game. Real-world discipline fuels your hero character, unlocking dungeon expeditions, armor forging, relic crafting, and guild raid boss battles.

## Core Features
1. **Guild Bounty Board**: Recurrence-based habit tracking (Daily, 3d, Weekly, 30d, Custom) with 3-tab layout (History, Active Quests, Upcoming Recurrences).
2. **Dungeon Expeditions**: Step-based and real-time dungeon exploration with monster codex, loot drops, and tactical combat.
3. **Guild Bazaar**: Armor forging (Tier 1-10 with layered avatar scaling), weapon masteries, relic crafting, and wardrobe.
4. **Guild Roster & Raid Bosses**: Multiplayer guild management, raid boss encounters, and Clerk organization integration.

## Design Non-Negotiables
- Cozy fantasy parchment aesthetic (warm amber gold, deep ink charcoal, sapphire blue accents, ruby crimson highlights).
- Every interactive control MUST provide tactile feedback (hover, active/pressed, focus, disabled).
- High visual contrast for legibility on parchment textures.
- Smooth transitions and splat particle animations for rewards and completions.
